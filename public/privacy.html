Privacy Policy for NightZone

Last updated: November 2026

1. What NightZone is

NightZone is a web portal (welcome gate) that introduces and links to its member websites: 303 Garden Station, Lumia 909 and Lovely Animals. NightZone does not sell products, does not take bookings and does not process payments. Each member website manages its own content, bookings and business, and has its own privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

NightZone does not require an account or a login to browse. We do not collect names, phone numbers, booking details or payment information on this hub. Basic, anonymous visit statistics (such as page views) may be collected by the hosting provider for the purpose of understanding traffic.

3. Links to member websites

When you click a link to a member website, you leave NightZone and are subject to that website's own privacy policy and terms. NightZone is not responsible for the data practices of member websites.

4. Data protection

We apply standard security measures to protect the limited data involved in operating this portal. We do not sell, trade or share personal information with external third parties.

5. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through one of the member websites linked from the NightZone homepage.
